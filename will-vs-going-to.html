📖 Quick Grammar Guide — When do we use WILL or GOING TO?

Use WILL / WON'T + verb

  • Decision right now, offer, or promise: "The phone is ringing! I'll answer it." / "I promise I won't tell."
  • Opinion or guess (I think, I'm sure, maybe, probably): "I think our team will win tomorrow."

Use AM / IS / ARE + GOING TO + verb

  • Plan already decided before speaking: "We bought tickets! We are going to see a concert."
  • Prediction with clear evidence right now (Look!): "Look at those dark clouds! It is going to rain."
